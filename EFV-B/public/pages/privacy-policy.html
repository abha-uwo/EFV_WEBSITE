Privacy Policy

Protecting your frequency and your data.

What We Collect

We only collect information necessary to serve you within the EFV™ ecosystem:

  • Name & email address (at registration)
  • Phone number (optional, for order updates)
  • Shipping address (for physical orders only)
  • Payment transaction references (no card details stored)
  • Reading & listening progress for your digital library
How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to enhance your Alignment Intelligence experience. We never sell or share your information with advertisers.

  • Process orders & deliver products
  • Send order confirmations & shipping updates
  • Provide access to your digital library
  • Send important platform announcements (opt-out anytime)
Data Security

All data is stored on encrypted, secured cloud infrastructure. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Payments are processed by certified gateways — we never see your card details.

Your data is encrypted at rest & in transit. We never sell your information.
Your Rights

You may at any time request a copy of your data, correct inaccuracies, delete your account, or opt out of communications — via our support channel.
